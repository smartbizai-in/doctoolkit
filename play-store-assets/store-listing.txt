PDF & Word tools, image converter, OCR scanner, and QR/barcode — all offline.

FULL DESCRIPTION
DocToolkit is a complete document toolkit that runs entirely on your phone — no
internet required, no account, no ads, nothing ever uploaded anywhere.

Convert, scan, and organize your PDFs, Word documents, and images without
sending a single file to someone else's server. Everything happens on-device.

📄 PDF TOOLS
• Images to PDF and PDF to images
• Merge multiple PDFs into one
• Organize — reorder, rotate, or delete pages
• Compress large PDFs
• PDF to Word, and Word to PDF

🖼️ IMAGE TOOLS
• Compress images without losing clarity
• Convert between JPG, PNG, and WebP

🔎 OCR — TEXT FROM PHOTOS
Point your camera at a printed page or photograph a document, and get back
editable text. Works fully offline — no cloud OCR service involved.

📷 QR & BARCODE SCANNER
Scan any QR code or barcode with your camera, or scan one from a photo already
in your gallery.

✨ CREATE QR CODES & BARCODES
Generate QR codes for text, links, WiFi passwords, UPI payments, contact
cards, email, SMS, or phone numbers. Generate barcodes in CODE128, EAN-13/8,
UPC, CODE39, and ITF-14.

🕘 HISTORY
A local record of what you've converted, so you can find and re-share a
result without redoing the work — kept only on your device.

Light, dark, and system theme support.

WHY OFFLINE MATTERS
Most "free" PDF/QR apps online quietly upload your files to a server to do the
actual conversion — which means your documents, ID photos, or business papers
pass through someone else's infrastructure. DocToolkit never does this. Every
tool — PDF conversion, OCR, image compression, QR generation — runs as code on
your own phone. There's no server for your files to reach, because DocToolkit
doesn't have one.

PERMISSIONS
• Camera — only for the QR/barcode scanner and OCR's "take a photo" option.
  Every other tool works with no camera access at all.
• Storage — to open the files you pick and save the files you create.
That's the complete list. No location, no contacts, no microphone.
